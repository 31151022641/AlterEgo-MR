Changes for AlterEgo MR:
The original full-body Blender characters are adapted to head portraits.
Geometry is evaluated, optimized, triangulated and converted to the app's
coordinate system. Materials are converted to opaque vertex colors and surface
finishes. Sampled facial-rig poses are converted into expression and gaze morphs.
The app also supplies optional palette changes and hairstyle visibility.
The adaptations are distinct from the original Blender production rigs.

Rain is the base of Rain Portrait and Holographic Matrix Human.
Snow is the base of Snow Portrait, Classical Golden Bust and Cyber-Synth Human.
